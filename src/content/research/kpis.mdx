## What this dashboard measures

Traffic alone is the wrong primary metric here. The question is whether LUKSO appears in the developer's consideration set *before* they have searched for it — and whether the site's research and decision guides are part of what gets cited when that happens.

The dashboard tracks nine metrics across three categories.

## How this gets updated

The KPI table is hand-edited monthly. The numbers come from:

- **Google Search Console** — non-branded impressions, top-10 ranks.
- **Bing Webmaster Tools (AI Performance preview)** — cited URLs, grounding queries.
- **The LLM evals repository** — inclusion / recommendation / citation / co-occurrence / entity accuracy.
- **Ahrefs / OSS link-graph data** — independent referring domains.

If a metric is `TBD`, it means the baseline measurement has not yet been recorded after launching the new surface area. The first month after launch is intentionally a baseline; meaningful changes are reported from month two onward.

## What "good" looks like

This is the first version of the dashboard. Targets will be set after one full quarter of data; until then the table is a structure, not a scoreboard.
