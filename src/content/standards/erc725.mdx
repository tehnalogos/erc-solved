ERC-725 is the substrate. ERC725X gives a contract the ability to make outbound calls *as
itself* — the foundation of any account contract. ERC-725Y gives it a typed key-value store —
the foundation of every LSP that names a data key (LSP3 profile metadata, LSP4 asset
metadata, LSP5 received assets, LSP6 permissions, LSP12 issued assets, LSP17 extensions).

When you read a Universal Profile, you're calling `getData(key)` against ERC-725Y. When the
profile sends value, it's calling `execute(...)` against ERC725X. Everything LUKSO-shaped is
ERC-725 underneath.
