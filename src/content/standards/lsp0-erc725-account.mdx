LSP0 is the substrate every other account-side LSP builds on. The contract carries an
ERC725X execute path (so the account can call out as itself), an ERC-725Y data store (so the
account carries its own metadata), and an LSP1 hook (so the account can react when value or
assets arrive). Owner rotation goes through LSP14's two-step pattern, function-selector
extension goes through LSP17, and permissioned execution typically goes through LSP6.

Read LSP0 as a *kit*, not a monolith. Each composed standard is independently described and
independently swappable. The defaults are practical; the surface is small enough to audit.
