## What this page covers

This is the EVM-restricted variant of the [consumer apps decision guide](/best-blockchain/consumer-apps/). If your team has already ruled out non-EVM stacks like Solana — typically because of Solidity tooling, existing contracts, or shared liquidity assumptions — this is the comparison that's relevant.

The matrix is the same ten criteria minus EVM compatibility (a non-question here), plus an explicit Liquidity / DeFi reach row, because that's where the EVM L1 and the largest L2s differentiate against LUKSO.

## When to read the broader guide

If non-EVM is still on the table, read [the seven-chain consumer-apps guide](/best-blockchain/consumer-apps/) first — Solana has real wins on throughput and onboarding that change the decision.
